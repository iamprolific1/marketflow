### 01 - Project Definition
- What exactly am I building?
(what marketplace is)
MarketFlow is a multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace designed to give small businesses a structured platform to showcase and manage their products while giving customers a more organized and transparent way to discover, evaluate, and purchase from vendors.

(who its for)
- small business owners
- customers
(problems it solves)
vendors need a dedicated place to showcase products
customers have to search across multiple platforms
vendors need better visibility into sales performance
(Goal)
The goal of MarketFlow is to create an online marketplace where vendors have a structured platform to reach customers and showcase their products, while customers can discover and purchase products in a more convenient and transparent way.
